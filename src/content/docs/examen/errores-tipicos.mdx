Estos son los errores que **más puntos hacen perder**. Si los evitás, ya tenés buena parte del
examen.

| # | ❌ Así no | ✅ Así sí | Por qué |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | She **play** tennis. | She **plays** tennis. | he/she/it → **-s** |
| 2 | My parents **works** here. | My parents **work** here. | *my parents* = they → sin -s |
| 3 | He **studys** a lot. | He **studies** a lot. | consonante + y → **-ies** |
| 4 | She doesn't **likes** it. | She doesn't **like** it. | con does/doesn't el verbo va **sin -s** |
| 5 | **Do** she live here? | **Does** she live here? | he/she/it → **does** |
| 6 | She **is go** to school. | She **goes** to school. | no mezclar *to be* con otro verbo en Simple |
| 7 | She **cooking** now. | She **is cooking** now. | Continuous = **is + -ing** (las dos piezas) |
| 8 | I **always am** tired. | I **am always** tired. | con *to be*, el adverbio va **después** |
| 9 | I **get usually** up at 7. | I **usually get** up at 7. | con los demás verbos, va **antes** |
| 10 | I **am wanting** a pizza. | I **want** a pizza. | *want, like, know…* no van con -ing |
| 11 | I don't **never** eat fish. | I **never** eat fish. | *never* ya es negativo |
| 12 | **Where she lives?** | **Where does she live?** | en las preguntas hace falta **does** |
| 13 | **Do you like pizza? — Yes, I like.** | **Yes, I do.** | respuesta corta con el ayudante |
| 14 | Quarter **to** eight = 8:15 | Quarter **to** eight = **7:45** | *to* = "para" |
