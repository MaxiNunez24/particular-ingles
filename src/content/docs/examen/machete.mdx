## 🧱 To be (ser / estar)

| + | − | ? |
|---|---|---|
| I **am** (I'm) | I'm **not** | **Am** I…? |
| he / she / it **is** | he **isn't** | **Is** he…? |
| you / we / they **are** | they **aren't** | **Are** they…? |

Para cómo es / cómo está / dónde está / edad. **No** para acciones: ~~She is go~~ → *She goes*.

## 🔁 Present Simple: rutinas, costumbres, cosas que son así

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| **+** | I **play** | she **plays** |
| **−** | I **don't play** | she **doesn't play** |
| **?** | **Do** you **play**? | **Does** she **play**? |
| **Resp. corta** | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. | Yes, she **does**. / No, she **doesn't**. |

**La -s:** casi todos **+s** · -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o **+es** (watches, goes, does) · consonante + y
→ **-ies** (studies) · vocal + y **+s** (plays) · have → **has**.

**La -s aparece UNA vez:** *she plays* ✔ · *she doesn't play* ✔ · ~~she doesn't plays~~ ✘

**Wh-:** What / Where / When / What time / How / Why + **do/does** + sujeto + verbo?
→ *Where **does** she **live**?*

## ⏰ Frecuencia

always (100%) › usually › often › sometimes › hardly ever › never (0%)

- **Antes** del verbo común: *I **usually get** up at 7.*
- **Después** de *to be*: *She **is always** late.*
- Expresiones **al final**: every day · once a week · twice a month · three times a year.
- Pregunta: **How often** do you…?

## 🎬 Present Continuous: lo que pasa AHORA

**am / is / are + verbo-ing**

| + | − | ? |
|---|---|---|
| **I'm** playing | **I'm not** playing | **Am** I playing? |
| she **is** playing | she **isn't** playing | **Is** she playing? — Yes, she **is**. |
| they **are** playing | they **aren't** playing | **Are** they playing? — No, they **aren't**. |

**-ing:** casi todos +ing · terminan en **-e** → se va (making) · consonante-vocal-consonante →
**doble** (running, swimming, sitting, getting).

## 🤔 ¿Cuál uso?

| 🔁 Simple | 🎬 Continuous |
|---|---|
| always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, at the weekend, once a week | now, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen! |

**Nunca con -ing:** like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand.

## 🕖 La hora y las preposiciones

- **at** + hora · at night · at the weekend
- **in** the morning / afternoon / evening · in July
- **on** Monday
- quarter **past** 7 = 7:15 · half past 7 = 7:30 · quarter **to** 8 = **7:45**
